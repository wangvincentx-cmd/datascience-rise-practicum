==============================================================================
  WARNING -- every out-of-sample AUC is BELOW 0.5, including the macro-only
  benchmark. That is not 'the press was wrong'; a specification that were merely
  uninformative would sit AT 0.5. Consistently-inverted forecasts across all
  specs point at the design: with 15 onsets in 64 years and an expanding window,
  each refit extrapolates from a handful of past recessions to a regime it has
  not seen. Report the in-sample fit and this instability together; do not
  quote the OOS AUC as a measure of press foresight.
  Read this against the claim-level models. A near-null at the claim level with
  real signal here would say the press held information in AGGREGATE that no
  individual forecast carried -- which is a finding about how to read newspapers,
  not a contradiction.
