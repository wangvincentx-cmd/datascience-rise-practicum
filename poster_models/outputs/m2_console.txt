=== how much of the variation is BETWEEN eras? ===
  between-block intercept SD : 0.512 (log-odds)
  intraclass correlation     : 0.074
  Reading: 7.4% of the residual variation in whether a forecast came true
  is variation between 3-year blocks, not between claims. That is exactly the
  correlation the naive standard errors assume away.
